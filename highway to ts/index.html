A slow-burn ASCII roguelike. You walk a highway in the middle of nowhere toward the town of TS.

The first 10 miles are guided: one new thing at a time, no surprises. Your first incoming car is practice — it can't kill you. After that, the highway gets meaner: strangers, hunger, and real traffic.

🚗 When a car screams in, a QUICK-TIME arrow flashes — press that arrow key in time or get SPLATTERED (30% mercy: crawl away at 1 HP).

to start you only need one key: W (walk). the rest unlocks as you go.

HOW TO WALK

▲ W / ↑ — step forward 1 mile. This is 90% of the game. Everything else can wait.

◀ ▶ A / D or ← / → — sidestep lanes (unlocks at mile 1).

✎ E — talk to whoever is roadside (unlocks at mile 2). Then 1–4 to pick dialogue.

🍖 F — eat a snack. Food drops 1 every 2 miles; starving hurts.

💊 C — use a fix-kit (+4 HP).

🚗 QTE: an arrow (◀ ▲ ▼ ▶) appears + alarm. Press THAT arrow key. Real cars splat — except a 30% miracle leaves you at 1 HP. Cars are rare and slow early, faster later.

🏁 Reach mile 100 to enter TS. Death is permanent; hi-score is saved.
